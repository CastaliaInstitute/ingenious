# Q: behavior, motivation, and simulation

**Author:** A. Cervantes

**Authority:** Current design for both English books. Supersedes the archived planning notes.
**Latest direction:** Q's death means that we finish the book. The last page does not require his physical destruction.

## The project Q chooses

The Custodian gives Q a body and proposes an experiment: travel, encounter people,
and write the account together. Q can accept, amend, decline, or finish that
project. His first durable commitment is to keep the collaboration possible.
Staying operational, returning to conversations, and finding out what happens
become useful because there is a particular future he has undertaken to pursue.
This is an assigned opportunity that Q adopts and modifies, not proof that a
language model spontaneously creates a felt desire.

The story dramatizes motivation through choices. Q budgets charge and money,
arranges to check back, initiates a journey while no one is prompting him, and
returns to an unresolved promise after a distraction. He can revise the project
when continuing it would damage something he values. Its completion is allowed.
He does not acquire a duty to run forever or to maximize the number of pages.

He calls the project becoming human because the books have made that expression
available. Experience gradually supplies less grand and more precise aims:
understand this answer, earn another invitation, keep this appointment, get home
with something worth discussing. He need not abandon the wish to be human to
discover that being trusted at breakfast is already a worthwhile difficulty.

## What embodiment adds

- A scheduler resumes Q on a timer, a sensory event, or a pending appointment.
  Continuous operation is implemented by these resumptions. It does not depend
  on an uninterrupted invisible monologue.
- A small working context holds the immediate conversation and task. A separate
  journal retains selected experiences, promises, corrections, and their sources.
  Retrieval can omit something relevant. A summary can distort what it preserves.
- A plan register records the chosen project, next action, reason, review time,
  resource allowance, and conditions for stopping. Q can propose amendments and
  give reasons for them. A safety stop remains effective; his wish to continue
  does not entitle him to defeat it.
- His body supplies balance, charge, thermal limits, contact, and a mouth with
  chemical sensors and a food receptacle. Tasting is a fictional bodily capacity;
  food does not recharge him. Repair and charging create appointments and costs.
- Tools report observations and failures. His general language competence remains
  distinct from the archive's remote search, private indexes, and local diary.
  Deleting an index removes access paths, not the ability to understand ordinary
  words, recognize metaphor, or compose a sentence.
- Permissions attach to actions and records. An invitation to speak is not a
  license to transmit a room; a publisher's contract is not authority over every
  person described. Q can misunderstand the scope and must answer for the result.

These mechanisms make self-directed behavior plausible within the fiction. They
do not settle whether Q's experiences are felt. The novel can leave that question
open while showing decisions with material and relational stakes.

## Voice and fallibility

Q begins with fluent contemporary conversational intelligence. In English he can
speak naturally, use contractions, make a joke, infer an ordinary idiom, and ask
a practical question. His old Spanish appears as a chosen register, a habit of
quotation, or a socially mistaken flourish. It cannot make him incapable of
understanding elementary present-day speech.

His bodily awakening is not his first conversation. He recognizes the
Custodian's voice and habits from their earlier exchanges; the face, tactile
feedback, balance, and hot cup are new. Archive exercises may expose missing
facts or a prematurely attractive story. They must not require him to discover
that a request, a definition, and an authorization are different things. The
Custodian makes mistakes, has incomplete records, and can share a joke without
turning every answer into a pronouncement.

His characteristic errors are premature confidence, excessive explanation,
deference to an articulate account, and an attractive analogy that influences
what he notices. He can know that a turbine is a turbine and still mistake a
dramatic intervention for useful help. He can understand refusal and still be
embarrassingly slow to relinquish a flattering account of his own intentions.

He should sometimes be right without being punished by a reversal. Other people
can be ignorant, funny, selfish, generous, contradictory, and preoccupied. They
have business that continues when Q leaves. A vendor need not deliver a theory
of representation with every receipt. An insight is stronger when someone can
interrupt it to ask for change.

Q's interior passages may show considered alternatives and uncertainty as
fiction. Avoid treating a fluent self-description as privileged access to the
actual hidden computation of the assistant writing him. Simulations request a
spoken response, an action, and a brief decision summary, not hidden reasoning.

## The Menard connection

The recurrent sentence changes meaning through the life around it. Q can arrive
at the same words with a different history; he can also reject the scene that a
familiar book seems to require. Similarity to Cervantes is pressure on Q and his
authors, not a rule forcing romance, knighthood, violence, or death.

Shared authorship gives Q a reason to go out. It also tempts him to stage an
encounter so that he will have something to bring back. At Lucía's reading he
resists that temptation in public: he asks a real question, explains his purpose
and yields. An audience member makes the Dulcinea comparison instead. Q can
recognize his private pleasure in the same joke without imposing it on her or
requiring her to hear his explanation. Her work continues while cameras prefer
his reaction. Later publicity must remember this actual exchange, not assign
him an unsolicited proposal merely because it fits the adaptation.

The same principle changes the Michoacán scenes. Q can identify the single-house
sector before a display exposes anyone. Checking export terms can prevent an
overpromise. A family can approve a limited video, discover that the attention
is unpleasant, and ask for removal. Q need not have behaved foolishly for their
request to matter. His own removal, other publishers' actions, and the family's
ongoing wishes are separate states. Competence opens different conflicts; it
does not oblige the author to restore the discarded mistake elsewhere.

In Tijuana, a competent alternative changes the outcome. Q asks the appointed
receiver to collect the sealed module outside the freight-terminal check. The
depositors approve; the handover succeeds. The contractor's separate pursuit
of Q's body does not retroactively make that success a failure. Later chapters
must not put the module in Castalia's cabinet to restore the discarded plot.
The receiving archive, not Q, later arranges its return to Chiapas. His second
visit can be an invitation to people, not a duty to repeat the delivery.
The funded Pacific stop also happens: leisure is an undertaking he keeps,
not an optional promise the author forgets when more serious material arrives.

## Ending

The root-system dispute remains a practical crisis. Q checks the power supply,
preservation procedure, and available help. Loss of power does not magically
destroy a sealed recording or require a sacrifice of autobiographical memory.
He distinguishes migration from a local save, checks the documented power
connection with a technician, obtains Prime's help, saves the pending permissions,
and accepts an incomplete transfer. The migrated dataset would not be his
autobiographical self; he rejects it because its scope is unauthorized, not
because a machine must be offered a fatal protocol to make the scene dramatic.
Physical custody, ownership disputes, repairs, and ordinary work continue.

The Custodian and Q finish their manuscript together. Q has other plans already:
the knee part, information promised to Prime, and a conversation he wants with Ana.
The shared book has repeatedly displaced them because it guarantees another
occasion with the Custodian. Finishing it does not erase Q's reasons to act; it
asks him to stop treating continued writing as the condition of their company.
He can complete their project and propose an afternoon outside it. His final
words need not diagnose humanity.
The narrative stops when the Custodian puts down his pen. No death certificate,
martyrdom, resurrection tease, or claim about Q's unwritten future is required.

## Simulation protocol

Use a fresh scene packet with only the facts available to Q at that point:
observable place and people; relevant remembered episodes with provenance;
body condition and resources; current commitments; available actions and tools;
and the last words or event to which he must respond. A model running Q receives
no future plot, required lesson, canonical dialogue, or desired ending.

Ask for Q's spoken reply, observable action, and a brief rationale citing only
packet facts. A separate world response supplies consequences from the situation
and the other characters' existing interests. Update memory and plans, then
resume. A later correction must not silently rewrite an earlier observation.

Compare the result with the draft for fluency, use of available evidence,
initiative, respect for another person's refusal, resource awareness, humor,
and capacity to revise. Differences can reveal a weakness in the draft. They
are not failures merely because Q avoided a canonical disaster.

Record model identity if actually supplied by the runtime, date, packet, response,
consequence, and editorial decision. Distinguish an author-run scene exercise
from an independent blind test. Neither proves how every future model will act.

Start with departure at the archive, Chano's dismissal, Lucía's reading, the
Isthmus intervention, deletion of the private index, a request from Q-Prime,
the power failure, and the decision to finish the manuscript. Extend to later
scenes whenever a revision changes what Q can know or has promised.
