# Chapter Thirteen: conversation has other work to do

## Scope and verdict

Review against `cf9f5d05`, both interludes included. This is an authorial close
reading, not a new independent faculty assessment or fresh-context model test.
The earlier `CHAPTER_13_VOICE_REVIEW.md` records the preceding substantive pass.

The donor negotiation and private writing call already give Q persuasive reasons
to act. He can support Lucía's project, object to being promised without being
asked, negotiate a bounded demonstration and keep an appointment he wants to
keep. He does not need a naive misunderstanding to make those interests conflict.
Rebeca and the Custodian share a joke that changes through its repetition. That
is a stronger Menard connection than an explanatory literary reference would be.

This pass preserves those sequences and repairs three small encounters. It does
not certify the chapter, much less both books, as A+ literature.

## Changes made

1. Ofelia's missing history: the narrator no longer explains that a new issue date
   is not proof of falsification after Q has already distinguished the dates.
   Ofelia arranges her receipts and says, "Fourteen years. They can spare another
   line." Her history gives her an answer of her own, not just evidence for Q.
2. The training form: a nurse physically covers the lower half of Q's draft and
   tests what an interrupted reader will retain. Q recognizes that he has put the
   explanation before the warning. They revise it and send it to the colleague
   checking the exercise. The scene shows competent revision without treating Q
   as a clinician or disclosing patient records. The examples remain fictionalized.
3. The comb: Q's abstract offer to disagree with memory becomes a joke about the
   price rising with each retelling. Alma keeps importance out of that dispute;
   Q accepts the distinction. He is participating in her conversation rather
   than diagnosing a defective human memory.

The result is 3,773 narrative words, 31 more than the prior layout. Greater
accessibility here comes from action and voices, not simply a smaller word count.
The two related outline entries are updated; the principal donor plot is unchanged.

  does not require finishing his life.

## Remaining literary questions

The chapter and its neighbors still concentrate on records, permission and repair.
The donor and coauthor scenes supply welcome friction, but a whole-book pass must
judge whether this institutional sequence lasts too long. Do not address that
question by manufacturing another breach merely to punish Q's competence.

Several private accounts are narrated with later permission. That is an explicit
fictional framing device, not independent evidence of how an actual archive would
handle testimony. The drawings are interpretations, not facsimiles of documents.

New dialogue in this pass is authored fiction, not a measured prediction of an
embodied model's response. Further representative fresh-context tests, whole-book
review and the remaining illustrated issues are still open. Production evidence
belongs in `ISSUE_13_PRODUCTION.md` once the finished proofs have been checked.
