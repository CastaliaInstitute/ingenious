# Paid companionship: the missing agreement

Date: 2026-09-08. Baseline: `2e9b26ed`.

## Finding and revision

Chapter Two formerly paid Chano for directions and dinner. Chapter Six offered
the "same daily pay," and Chapter Sixteen's testimony recalled a later paid
traveling agreement. The arrangement was stated in the character summary but
never made on the page. The four-call dismissal exercise in
`CHAPTER_06_FRESH_CONTEXT_REVIEW.md` prompted a source audit; it did not itself
establish that missing scene.

An eighty-word motel exchange now follows Q's call requesting another day.
Chano initiates the pay question. They agree a day's rate separate from meals
and fares; Q checks the card limit, records the amount and date, and sends a
copy. Further days require agreement. The joke about not being paid to agree
with Q preserves Chano's independence without converting their relationship
into a tutorial on employment. No numerical rate or account balance is invented.

The outline records the same sequence. Chapter Six's renewed offer and Chapter
Sixteen's testimony remain unchanged: they now have an earlier event to recall.
This addition does not make wages the entire explanation of their friendship.
Their later testimony deliberately retains pay, companionship and affection as
three compatible facts.
