## Verdict

The strongest passages combine the body's exact discomfort with ordinary
companionship: Chano refusing to be spoken about while he is standing there,
Omar adjusting a pad, and the Custodian bringing a chair. The principal weakness
was causal. Having learned of the recovery inquiry, a competent Q would ask
whether the archive could collect its own deposit outside the equipment check.
The draft treated the original itinerary as destiny. This pass accepts a plausible
alternative and changes the later plot, while preserving the contractor's separate
pursuit of Q. It also fulfills the Pacific plan instead of reducing travel to
continuous service. This is a substantial revision, not an A+ certification.
