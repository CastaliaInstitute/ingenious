## Finding and revision

After the successful off-site delivery, the draft left Q waiting at a café
because Elena's adviser wanted to verify a notice. The contractor was visibly
nearby. Nothing established why a telephone inquiry required remaining there.
An eloquent character is not necessarily an agent if he never asks that question.

Q now asks whether the adviser can call them at their room. The adviser has not
confirmed a requirement to stay at that café and asks them to remain reachable.
Chano takes out the keys; Q closes his bag and sends the Custodian their intended
address; Elena has her food wrapped. Salas arrives before they reach the door.
The outline and custody record reflect this changed decision.

This is a local repair, not a newly successful escape. Salas's timing remains an
authored world event. The tests do not demonstrate that custody is inevitable,
that the fictional order is legally sound, or that the whole sequence is free
of plot convenience. The separate, completed deposit delivery still succeeds;
the module does not reappear among the retained papers or Q's transport items.

The existing knee warning is already explicit. It remains in the manuscript:
the technician activates restraint before support is placed. Neither a fluent
warning nor a model's asserted physical action establishes what another person
will do. There is no need to make Q foolish to permit someone else's mistake.
