# Chapter Five: pacing and counter-voices

Revision date: 2026-09-08. Baseline: `014f3956`.

## Verdict

The chapter's strongest feature is the friction between a useful public project
and the private lives it cannot summarize: Lidia wants to leave for her match,
vendors want different things, and an uncle wants to hear his own song. Its
greatest threat was repetition: three adjacent encounters could read as another
collection, objection, and removal procedure. This pass differentiates their
actions and gives Q pleasure and an imperfect account of himself. It improves
this chapter's range; it is not evidence of whole-book A+ quality.
