# Chapter Eleven: answer the child before the claim

Date: 2026-09-08. Baseline: `56075715`.

## Finding and revision

The private orange-peeling meeting already gives Q and Prime curiosity, humor
and a relationship beyond the stage format. This pass preserves it. The weaker
exchange was Q's answer to the child's question about paying for the two damaged
cartons. He explained the scope of liability without first answering whether he
had paid. Precision sounded like evasion, especially before a child.

Q now begins with "Not yet." The boy asks what was in the cartons; Q cannot
retrieve a fact Lety never supplied. The boy's returned-television story gives
him an interest other than extracting the novel's moral. Q asks whether the
replacement arrived instead of continuing his defense. After the event he sends
his adviser a request for the contents and separate cost, then puts other messages
aside for Prime. This is an initiated action, not an invented completed payment.
The separate cost and unpaid state are now explicit authorial facts; the earlier
chapter left them unspecified. The disputed broader claim and later hearing
remain unresolved. No object is invented inside either carton.

The settlement passage loses repeated explanations already carried by dialogue.
It now explicitly distinguishes the promised transport inventory from the
underlying correspondence Esteban will not promise. Q leaves that request open.
The guard asking his next destination after signing shows continued restrictions;
the narrator need not explain that signing has not made custody voluntary.
Q's unasked question about audience size gives vanity a small observable form.
He also answers Chano's shoulder joke as a joke, without asking whether it is a
literal request. These are authorial revisions, not verbatim model outputs.
