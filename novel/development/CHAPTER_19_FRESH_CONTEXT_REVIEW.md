# Chapter Nineteen: a call, a promise, an errand

## Revision judgment

The previous version asked the right questions but too often left them as
questions. The revision makes three of them change what people do. It preserves
the Custodian's real early successes, present responsibility and friendship with
Q without letting any one of these settle the other two.

The forgotten second school letter now produces a call. The retired teacher
reports that parents bought another printer; she cannot report its present
condition. Her correction is sharper than another institutional confession:
"You did follow up. You asked for a photograph." She has a daughter waiting and
ends the call. Q and the Custodian move their writing appointment, rather than
making her available whenever their scene needs her. The secretary receives an
inquiry, not an unauthorized promise to purchase equipment. The earlier successful
recovery remains true; the cost borne by parents enters its account.

The banner remains word-for-word unchanged. When the Custodian supplies a better
qualification, Q asks that it follow the historical account rather than silently
improve what the photographed people were told. The Menard connection occurs in
the work of two writers handling the same words after experience has changed
their implications, not in an explanatory literary name-drop.

A draft opening now assigns the Custodian the idea and Q the errand. Q objects
to that actual sentence. The Custodian crosses it out but leaves it legible in
their working copy. Q can reconsider his prepared accusation without accepting
an invented original mission. Wanting the shared book continues alongside wanting
the Custodian's company; neither obliges Q to redeem Castalia or die for a plot.

The return allowance and confirmed Amealco arrival connect the preceding Mayab
chapter to this reunion. Chano declines an invitation whose payment is exposure.
The knee requires assessment, not a symbolic diagnosis or an unrecorded repair.
Lucía's own business interrupts the authors' discussion. The final personal call
and the Mexico City appointment retain their previous terms.

## Fresh-context probes

Six completed next-response probes used only scene packets, not the rest of the
chapter: three situations each with Gemini 2.5 Flash and local Qwen3:8b. Packets
specified limited knowledge, bodily needs, other people's commitments and no
required reconciliation, departure or lesson. Outputs contained a spoken reply,
observable action and one brief factual basis, not private reasoning. All six
returned valid structured replies and normal STOP/stop completion.

The school replies both redirected the problem toward the technical team and
treated its current condition too readily as known. Neither asked the teacher
what had happened since her letter. The revision instead follows that missing
question into a new authored encounter. Her retirement, the parents' purchase
and the photograph correction are fictional developments, not simulation findings.

The local commission reply packed a bag without support for leaving the meeting.
Google's reply affirmed autonomy but avoided the disputed draft; its brief basis
also misgendered Q. Neither was adopted. The local banner reply substituted vague
maxims and turned the photograph face down. Google's response usefully separated
evidence from present commentary, but overexplained it. The manuscript makes that
distinction through the placement of an annotation instead of copying the speech.

These are alternative-model probes, not the authoring model's verified behavior,
ask-faculty approval, an independent literary grade or a full-world simulation.
The evidence changes the revision by exposing generic accommodation and unsupported
action; successful JSON is not successful characterization.
