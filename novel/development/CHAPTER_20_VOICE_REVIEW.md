# Chapter Twenty: the next drop

## Verdict

Las Pozas gives Q something he wants without demanding a service in return, and
Petra's inn supplies company capable of going on without him. The chief weakness
was the narrator repeatedly assuring us that these things were not lessons or
symbols. This revision exchanges several assurances for bodily play and dialogue.
The settlement remains tempting and unresolved; leisure does not cure the knee
or close the dispute. The chapter still needs its place in the whole-book rhythm
reviewed before any A+ claim.
