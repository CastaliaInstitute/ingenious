# Chapter Four: publicity, company and the mine

## Verdict

The chapter's strongest conflict is Q knowingly buying another opportunity to
speak from people who have already falsified his speech. His pleasure in an
audience, wish to continue the journey and concern for Chano do not line up
neatly. The principal threat remains repetition: if every encounter becomes
a good sentence clipped into a bad advertisement, the reader can anticipate
the mechanism before the people have finished speaking. This pass makes the
company less uniform, restores ordinary exchanges and repairs the causal
record. It does not certify the whole novel as A+.

## Major findings and revisions

### 1. Competence must change what Q actually says

File: `novel/THE_INGENIOUS_MACHINE.md`, Chapter Four and both interludes.
Label: `SCENE_AS_ARGUMENT`.
The old correction named Chano in administrative language without saying what
he had done. The objection to a fabricated quote became an abstract maxim.
The miner's bereavement immediately prompted a request for quotation. These
exchanges made Q a device for setting up the next correction.

Q now credits Chano with finding the exit, challenges the fabricated passage
directly and asks that it be removed. At the ledger he waits and redirects
the lamp; Hilario initiates the private memory and sets the public scope. Q's
useful wage-comparison question receives a useful answer. These changes retain
the consequential mistake in the linked employment profile and the compromised
tour contract; competence is not converted into perfection.

Priority: major. Revised; independent behavioral validation remains outstanding.

### 2. The people need attractions beyond their function

File: Chapter Four, backstage and ledger scenes.
Label: `INSTITUTION_FLAT` / `SANCHO_FUNCTIONAL`.
Estela formerly offered only polished manipulation, and Chano explained what
her greeting meant. The miner supplied a theory of silence in response to a
reasonable question. This left the counter-voices suspiciously well equipped
to teach the novel and less able to surprise it.

Estela now moves the chair away from a cable and checks Q's comfort. Her
consideration is compatible with her subsequent intrusion, and Q's liking her
makes the interview harder. Chano joins an unfinished tune while a cat delays
traffic. Hilario remembers a brother who stole his lunch. Mar forwards the
removal request while Beto asks about metrics: one action is not a cure for the
other, but the staff no longer speak with one mouth.

Priority: major. Revised; sustain the distinction in later scenes.

### 3. Later evidence must remember the revised encounter

File: Chapter Four's final leak, audience interlude and ledger coda;
Chapter Sixteen's testimony; `novel/OUTLINE.md`.
Label: `SCENE_AS_ARGUMENT`.
The leak still attributed the discarded speech to Lucía. Trial testimony still
said Q had proposed a role and withdrawn it. The ledger coda credited Chano
alone for a distinction that Hilario had explicitly required. The platform
episode needed to occur after the agreement that enabled its profile link.

The leak and trial now recall the audience's comparison, Lucía's refusal,
Q's private nickname and his later public naming. Ledger testimony distinguishes
reading in place, the book remaining in the office, and selected pension figures
going with Hilario. A short interview form precedes the performance; the longer
tour agreement follows the mine visit; Q finally reads and posts to the thread
the next morning. The outline records these separate events.

Priority: major. Named contradictions corrected; broader cross-book checking
remains necessary.

## Minor repairs

- Replace the orphaned “He meant” transition and a backstage answer with a
  missing antecedent; retain humor rather than merely deleting it.
- Use Teatro Juárez's geometric ceiling, star chandelier and horseshoe hall
  instead of an unsupported allegorical ceiling. The primary source and limits
  are recorded in `REVISION_RESEARCH.md`.
- Distinguish a historic brake lever on display from the lift's actual controls.
  The later fault remains an invented incident, not an assertion about a real
  tourist mine. Q does not knowingly descend with no usable safety controls.

## Passages to protect

1. Q wants another turn at the microphone even after understanding the contract.
2. Chano's trumpet note and Hilario's stolen lunch are not lessons for Q.
3. The corrected deduction is useful without publicity or a solved pension case.

After revision, Q can be articulate, considerate and useful while still wanting
the wrong bargain badly enough to sign it.
