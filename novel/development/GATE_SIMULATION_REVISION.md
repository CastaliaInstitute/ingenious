Date: 2026-09-08. Revision baseline: `bd196da8`.

## Verdict

over publicity, access and responsibility. The fresh-context probes identified
nearby staff help as a plausible alternative to his personal boundary crossing.
The revised source takes that alternative seriously: the guard holds the gate,
Q stays in the bay, and a photographer pursues his own composition. This is an
editorial decision after rereading the source, not proof that two model samples
determine what must happen. The local land and labor disputes remain independent
of both Q's competence and his fame.

## Three findings and revisions

1. **Available help must be allowed to work.** Chapter Eight had a guard at the
   latch but made Q hold the gate, cross the boundary and later say he should
   have called the guard. Q now makes that request first. No invented refusal,
   delay or greater peril restores the discarded act. The woman clears the gate.
2. **Give the photographer his own motive.** He asks Q to turn for a picture;
   Q wants Teresa's answer about opening hours first. The man leaves to find an
   angle containing both Q and a turbine. He does not follow Q onto the track.
   Teresa's precautionary group stop remains her operational decision. This
   preserves fame's effect on other people without calling their choices Q's
   physical intervention.
3. **Later knowledge must follow the new scene.** Q sees the visitor leave the
   bay but loses sight of him. Lucía supplies the instruction masked by wind.
   The next-morning staff report supplies the subsequent route. The site plan
   marks where Q stayed, and both Chapter Sixteen accounts agree. The old
   confession of crossing is removed rather than retained as a moral refrain.

Protect the archive's power-purchase relationship, the disputed opening hours,
and Bixha's independent work. Q remains capable of a poor proposal later at the
meeting; one successful practical request does not settle his character.

## Scope and consequences

Only Chapters Eight and Sixteen change in the manuscript. Chapter Ten's return
journey still distinguishes the Isthmus complaint from the body-custody notice;
its passage does not assert a physical crossing and needs no rewrite. Chapter
Seventeen's altered entrance and unfinished work remain possible after a visitor
incursion. Neither event now depends on Q having entered the internal track.

The guarded passage, photographer's choice, operational response and eventual
opening hours remain authored events. The fresh-context tests did not simulate
wind-field controls, validate local law, or prove how a guard would respond.
They supplied a plausible alternative, not a prewritten canonical result.
