## Verdict

Yadira's radio stories already give the chapter humor, conflicting needs and
people who have lived before Q arrives. Its greatest weakness is the narrator's
habit of certifying Q's competence after every sensible act. That commentary
can make an eloquent traveller feel like a demonstration of good procedure.
This pass replaces some certification with disagreement, pleasure and a decision
that can be declined. The chapter still contains many document exchanges;
improvement is not an A+ certification or a whole-book rhythm verdict.
