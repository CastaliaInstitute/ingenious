# Chapter Twelve: time spent for pleasure

## Scope and verdict

Baseline: `a0565037`. Chapter Twelve and all three interludes were read in full,
alongside the earlier voice review and exact-facing plan. This is an authorial
review, not an external faculty assessment or an A+ certification.

The earlier revision already gave Q work to finish and conversations he wanted.
Its remaining weakness was explanatory pressure: after people acted, the narrator
often explained why their action did not imply some larger claim. Necessary
uncertainties about records and exposure were becoming the rhythm of the fiction.
The current pass removes selected repetitions and gives Q an encounter whose
pleasure does not depend on being corrected or delivering a service.

  distinct from the pending permissions/cache review; neither removal nor a

  That action carries her continued interest without the narrator explaining

  boy then borrows Q's voice to request more time. Q is socially fluent, amused

- The teacher's paired script lines replace a closing moral about the performance.

Micaela remains the tianguis child, not Lidia. Her complaint records, the parents'
different instructions, Chano's retained ledger, the restricted material supplied
to Prime, the unresolved technical route and the accompanied-travel arrangement

work to do. The Custodian remains a tall, long-haired, bespectacled man.

The new voice game is authored, not independently simulated. Earlier fresh-context
tests elsewhere in the novel do not validate this exchange or establish an actual
assistant's bodily capacities. No new model or ask-faculty calls were made for
this pass. Q's vocal imitation is a fictional ability shown in the scene.

## Remaining criticism

The loading bay, charter, school and FAQ still concentrate procedural subjects.
Those subjects belong to the plot, but whole-book review must test their cumulative
weight against travel, desire and surprise. A lively final exchange does not by
itself solve that structural concern. Jacinto and Micaela also need to retain their
independence in later references, rather than become stock correctors of Q.

The user's ending clarification remains authoritative: finishing the book is not
a requirement to kill Q. No ending change is necessary in this pass; the source
