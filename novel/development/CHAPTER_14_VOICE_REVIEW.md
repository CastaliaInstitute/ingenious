# Chapter Fourteen: dispatch, dismissal and lunch

## Verdict

The sinking chair is the chapter's strongest comic object: it reveals Chano's
vanity without invalidating his useful work. The main narrative was less vivid,
summarizing errors, corrections and political pressure in administrative language.
This pass gives those events speakers and exchanges. Q contributes remotely
without taking over Chano's episode. These changes improve the reading experience;
they do not certify A+ literary quality or complete the whole-book review.

collaborative route check; make Irma own the suspension and Chano own his return.

Protect Nereida's heavy proof, Leticia's page-two objection and the chair's
eventual repair. Neither success nor vanity has to erase the other.

After revision Chano has an administration with partial achievements and people
he must answer, followed by a meal he would like to eat.
