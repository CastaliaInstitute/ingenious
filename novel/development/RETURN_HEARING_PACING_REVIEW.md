Date: 2026-09-08. Baseline: `612e88b4`. Author: A. Cervantes.

## Verdict and scope

The strongest passages in Chapters Ten and Sixteen make large disputes pass
through particular bodies and working lives: mango stains Q's sleeve, Omar
adjusts the pad, Hilario interrupts testimony to sell water, and Chano says Q
is his friend. The greatest threat was the narrator's repeated explanation of
distinctions those actions and replies had already made intelligible. This
pass keeps the cases and outcomes while reducing commentary and consolidating
Q's arrival forms. It does not turn a custody objection into consent or a hearing
into an acquittal of everything. This is a full paired chapter review with
their interludes, not a fresh complete reading of both books or an A+ grade.

The local Castalia monitor and Q design govern the editorial decisions. No
external faculty response, new model probe, cultural field check, or legal
review is represented as having taken place. The institutional procedure
remains fictional rather than guidance about an actual Mexican proceeding.
