# Chapter Fifteen: the question can remain open

## Verdict

The chapter correctly separates Q's language and personal memory from a revoked
research tool. Its weakness is the amount of narration explaining that separation
and its ethical significance. This revision gives the cost a task and a delay,
lets the Custodian share the temptation of convenience, and allows ordinary
conversation to continue after the review. The improvement is literary movement,
not an A+ certification or independent proof of model behavior.
