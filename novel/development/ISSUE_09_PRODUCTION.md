and the outline entry and northern-journey continuity were checked. No manuscript
or outline change was needed for this production pass. The ending remains
"The Custodian put down his pen." Finishing the account need not finish Q's life.

leaks. He retains the signature page and does not let Q refuse on his behalf.

the paragraph about narrative time delays the shoes. Before accepting the sealed

arrival scene in this issue.

compulsory literary destiny. The radio bulletin is jointly made; the aunt finds
the child. Q can help without becoming the source of everyone else's knowledge.

bounded check does not settle whole-book rhythm or establish an A+ grade. No
fresh independent model simulation or external faculty review was run in this
production pass. Earlier authored alternatives are described in
`CHAPTER_09_VOICE_REVIEW.md` and the scene exercises.
