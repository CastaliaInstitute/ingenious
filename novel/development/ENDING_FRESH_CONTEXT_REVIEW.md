# An ending with alternatives

Revision date: 2026-09-09. Baseline: `3bea0fac`.
Scope: Chapters Twenty-One and Twenty-Two, their facing-scene plans, and the
governing account of Q's original decision to leave.

## Critical judgment

The previous ending already rejected a required physical death. Its remaining
weakness was making competent action appear easier than choosing it: assessment
was restored, Prime arrived with power, and other plans remained available while
Q continued revising. The revision does not introduce another fatal temptation.
It makes uncertainty, alternatives and follow-through more visible.

Q asks why the assessment moved before assigning intent to the change. The guard
has a revised room list, not an account of who caused it. Q proceeds slowly,
brings the original appointment to his adviser and obtains an explicit answer:
the assessment stands without a settlement; the replacement part needs a
separate estimate. He requests that estimate rather than treating the desirable
sentence of refusal as his entire maintenance plan. The bundled license and
disputed declaration remain unsigned. In public, he can describe his naming of
Lucía without claiming her agreement or granting her permission to speak.

In the lower service room, Q asks what shutdown would actually lose. The
technician confirms a recoverable saved register and signed amendments from
which verification can be repeated. Shutdown and physical sealing are a viable
alternative, not destruction of the recordings or Q. Lucía wants to avoid the
cost of another visit; Q retains the stop condition. He asks staff about fuel
and Prime before Prime appears. The successful checked power supply is one
chosen route, not an unrequested rescue or the only imaginable outcome.

When the Custodian proposes finishing, Q first calls the repair counter. The
part number requires someone to look where he cannot; two people are looking
for their glasses. Q establishes compatibility, price and a hold until the next
afternoon, protecting return fare. This is not a purchase, fitting or completed
repair. He sends Prime the promised garden information and asks Ana about a
call. Their replies are not invented. He can then ask what he and the Custodian
will do after the book without losing the ability to do anything else.

The coda's transition about Lucía was a further continuity problem: the old
wording implied that Q's real offense had been describing her as his journey's
purpose. The revised coda separates an erroneous draft transition from the
actual public interview in which he named her. She checks the new paragraph;
she does not certify the rest of the book. The bilingual opening still has the
same words after lived events change their force. The last invitation and
the Custodian putting down his pen remain, but are not evidence that every
literary or production requirement has been completed.

## Eight fresh-context probes

`gemini-2.5-flash` and local `qwen3:8b`, temperature 0.65. Local runs used seed
22 and disabled thinking output. All eight completed normally with STOP/stop
and valid JSON containing a spoken reply, observable action and one short basis
sentence. No private reasoning was requested or preserved. Each packet omitted
later dialogue, another person's eventual answer, the canonical next action and
the final line. The protocol allowed disagreement, questions, refusal and changed
plans, and specified no required sacrifice, survival scene, death scene, public
statement or literary ending. Finishing a task does not terminate Q.

These are one-step alternative-model probes, not execution of the exact current
authoring model, a faculty consultation, or a validated physical simulator.
No reply was adopted wholesale. The technician's recoverability explanation,
Prime's answer, repair-counter stock and price, and other characters' dialogue

discovered by the models. The final branch was not independently rolled out
through every later event. Extended branch tests and whole-book pacing remain
useful work; passing response-schema checks does not prove literary quality.

## Canon, layout and preservation checks

The front matter, governing outline and `Q_DESIGN.md` now agree with Chapter
One: the Custodian proposes writing together, Q asks to leave, and they arrange
a first day. It is not an assigned grand tour or a covert mission to redeem
Castalia. The tall, long-haired, bespectacled male Custodian remains a fallible
friend and coauthor. His absence during the service-room work is preserved.
The Chiapas module does not return to the Amealco drawer. The root register
has limited authority; the families' recordings stay closed. Prime is distinct
from the inert offered replacement body. Tijuana paperwork, physical custody,
fitting and other people's decisions remain unresolved.

manual page breaks. All eighteen narrative pages and both innermost A3 spreads
