## Verdict

The opening is strongest when ordinary company interrupts Q's literary ambition:
the broken breakfast knife, the interrupted poem, Chano's verifiable lunch in
León, and a vendor whose ice bill outlasts the legend. Its greatest remaining
threat is authorial compulsion. A protagonist described as conversationally
capable must not lose that capacity whenever the Quixote correspondence needs
him to offend someone. This review covers Chapters 2-3 and the related Chapter
16 evidence repair, not a new whole-book grade or an independent faculty verdict.

## Revision brief

Three major revisions, in order:

1. Let a fresh-context reading scene challenge the Dulcinea comparison itself.
2. Cut repeated procedural explanation while retaining causal distinctions.
3. Audit later memories and pictures against the authorized entry and blocked view.

Three passages or devices to protect:

1. Chano's food, work, vanity and kindness, including his own register joke.
2. Lucía and the municipal workers acting when Q is absent.
3. The ordinary unfinished life beyond the Custodian's final sentence.

After revision, the novel should follow a capable, fallible companion whose
encounters generate the book without being obliged to provide it with lessons.
