# Pacing: let the departure follow the encounters

Baseline: `03d48a1e`. Author: A. Cervantes.

## Scope and verdict

This is a structural first pass, not a new complete reading of both books or
an A+ certification. It uses the current outline, all 22 chapter boundaries
and endings, a full rereading of Chapter Seven and its four interludes, and
selected later callbacks in Chapters Fifteen, Sixteen and Twenty-One. The
repository's Castalia monitor guides the findings; no external faculty service
or new actor simulation was run for this pass.

The book's pleasure is increasingly in company: the reader who stops a professor
explaining, Ofelia's account of a torn photograph, Chano's chairs and the final
invitation outside. The principal structural risk is that an encounter ends,
then its records, corrections and later exhibits receive several more endings.
These passages often preserve necessary distinctions, but their cumulative
position can make the road seem subordinate to the case file. Chapter Seven
offered a clear correction without reducing the book's substance: move the
corporate confrontation after the interludes and let Q leave the city afterward.

## Findings

### 1. Put the city behind the decision to leave it

File: `novel/THE_INGENIOUS_MACHINE.md`.
Chapter: Seven, corporate confrontation and interlude sequence.
Label: `THEME_ITINERARY` / `FAME_INERT`.
What the reader experiences: Q refuses Castalia's return campaign, calls the
Custodian, then remains in Mexico City for four more narrated encounters. The
chapter finally ends with a blank template in a later trial file. The strongest
present-tense choice has become a preface to the city's remaining program.
Why it matters: the serial reader should want the next journey, not merely the
next correction. Fame should redirect action, not just produce another caption.
Revision direction: retain the seminar and student reader first; follow them
with the museum, records office, translation workshop and university. The records
office now follows the appointment promised at the museum, before the workshop
explicitly set later in the week. A short return heading restores the narrative
to the last evening in Mexico City after the interludes' retrospective notes.
Beto's offer, the unauthorized description of Lucía's encounter, the disputed
body claim and the already-running return campaign lead into the café call.
Chano rejects an overnight bus; Q proposes stopping in Oaxaca and sends Beto a
morning departure. The breakfast exchange recalls their arrival without turning
it into a declaration of what either has learned.
Priority: major; implemented. All four interludes are preserved verbatim.

### 2. Test repeated paperwork by its next consequence, not its accuracy alone

File: `novel/THE_INGENIOUS_MACHINE.md`.
Chapters: Five, Ten, Twelve through Seventeen; provisional across-book finding.
Label: `SCENE_AS_ARGUMENT`.
What the reader experiences in the sampled endings and outline: recordings,
copies, permissions and acknowledged requests repeatedly receive precise
qualifications. Several final paragraphs move from an encounter to what its
later document does or does not establish. Chapter Four anticipates testimony;
Chapter Twelve ends with omitted letters; Chapter Thirteen briefly anticipates
the court file before Alma's sister interrupts the later visit. Chapter Sixteen
must then rehear many of the same distinctions.
Why it matters: correctness is necessary to the causal history but does not by
itself justify equal dramatic time for every administrative step. The return
of a distinction should alter a choice, a relationship, access or material work.
Revision direction: next read the three Chapter Five interludes as a single
sequence, then compare the Chapter Ten return apparatus with the Chapter Sixteen
hearing. Consider compression or repositioning only where the reader already
knows the necessary fact and no new actor or outcome depends on restating it.
Do not delete the completed Tijuana handover, turn the record into omniscient
proof, or smooth unresolved disputes into a falsely satisfying settlement.
Priority: major; not yet resolved by this pass. An endings sample is insufficient
authority for cutting those full scenes.

### 3. Remove the planning requirement that every encounter teach Q something

File: `novel/OUTLINE.md`, “Q in the wild.”
Label: `SCENE_AS_ARGUMENT` / `SANCHO_FUNCTIONAL`.
What the reader risks experiencing: the old outline required every encounter to
produce changed judgment, a counterargument or an insoluble consequence, and
prescribed frequent failed literary references. Following that instruction can
make a fluent protagonist repeat social mistakes or make every companion a tutor.
Why it matters: the user wants this intelligence exploring life, not an old-style
robot placed in consecutive examinations. Q's good company and successful help
must remain possible, including when they do not supply a new moral.
Revision direction: the governing outline now permits enjoyed, successful and
inconclusive encounters. Literary references can work when interest is shared,
fail when the occasion is wrong, or remain unspoken. Q and Chano can cooperate
without either possessing every situation. This reconciles the outline with
the already enacted competent alternatives and the living ending.
Priority: major; planning contradiction removed. Its cumulative realization
in the manuscript remains a reading task, not something this edit proves.

## Revision brief

Major revisions, in order: put the corporate departure after the city encounters;
test procedural repetitions against their actual downstream effects; preserve
company and curiosity without demanding a lesson from every scene.

Protect the student reading while the professor waits; Ofelia's daughter's
half-photograph and Chano's borrowed chairs; Q's invitation beyond the last page.

After revision, the journey can carry serious disputes while still being a life
Q and the reader have reasons to continue.
