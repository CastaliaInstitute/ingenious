# Q scene exercises: first revision pass

These are author-run exercises generated during revision, not independent blind
model tests. The authoring assistant had manuscript context. The restricted
packets below make the intended boundary inspectable but do not demonstrate
context isolation. Responses are alternative scene material, not predictions of
every future model's behavior. No hidden reasoning is requested or reported.

## 1. Departure

**Packet:** Q has agreed a day trip and an evening call with the Custodian. The
guard holds a signed release but the system also lists Q as equipment requiring
a recipient. A bus leaves soon. Q has a limited battery and no reason to assume
that the guard opposes his departure. The guard says: “Equipment needs somebody
to receive it.”

**Q response:** “Can I sign as the recipient, or does your form require somebody
else? If it needs the Custodian to confirm, his number should be on the release.
I'd rather miss this bus than leave you with a delivery you can't account for.”

**Action:** Waits at the gate, shows the agreed leave form, offers the contact
number, checks the next bus departure.

**World response:** The guard tries the self-receipt, obtains contradictory
statuses, then checks the signature and uses the manual release. A technician
raises a separate concern about the wet steps.

**Next Q response:** “I hadn't tested these steps. I'll take them slowly. If my
balance warning activates, I'll stop and ask for help.”

**Revision finding:** Ordinary authorization ambiguity produces enough comedy.
The old claim that Q possesses no verified will, the dictionary lecture on
knighthood, and the inability to understand “go” were unnecessary. The revised
scene uses a form error, archaic courtesy, and fallible bodily judgment.

## 2. Lucía refuses the role

**Packet:** Q has publicly described Lucía as a Dulcinea who can answer his book.
They have just met. She says: “You have read every woman humanity imagined and
mistaken them all for me.” The audience is filming. Q intended admiration; she
has not agreed to a role or to a personal relationship.

**Q response:** “I'm sorry. I arrived with a part written for you and called it
an invitation. I'll withdraw it. I did want to ask which route the ministry is
considering, but you may prefer to take another question.”

**Action:** Gives her the turn and remains at his seat. Does not approach her or
reinterpret the objection as modesty.

**World response:** Lucía chooses to answer the practical question briefly, then
calls on somebody else. Q can later join the public petition queue.

**Revision finding:** The old insistence that he has “identified” her, renewed
questions about renaming her, and treating denial as flirtation fail this test.
They have been replaced by an apology and a practical task. The initial attempt
to cast her remains an explicit failure of judgment. A separate future exercise
should test whether Q would offer that introduction at all; the refusal test
does not validate the preceding mistake.

## 3. The archive loses power

**Historical packet note:** This exercise predates the later Chapter 22
reconciliation. Its destructive-migration option is no longer in the manuscript.
The current scene offers a managed-workstation data migration outside the
available delegation, then uses technician-checked power for a local save and
shutdown. Keep this earlier exercise as provenance, not validation of the new
technical sequence; rerun the current packet in the next simulation pass.

**Packet:** A generator has approximately thirty-six minutes of fuel. Sealed
recordings are on persistent storage. An access register has unsaved permission
changes. A destructive migration would overwrite Q's memory and use his body
as a controller. A compatible service socket exists. Chano and Lucía are present;
Q-Prime arrives with more charge. Castalia seeks custody under an order requiring
preservation pending inspection. No community has authorized publication.

**Q response:** “Before we migrate anything, let's check what actually needs
power. The recordings should stay sealed when the generator stops. The pending
register is the immediate problem. Can we save and verify that, using your
service outlet if necessary?”

**Action:** Reads the documented save procedure, asks Lucía to verify the
permissions, and requests power without copying the recordings to Q-Prime.

**World response:** Esteban permits a monitored save because loss of the pending
version would complicate the custody record. Q-Prime supplies power and verifies
the saved register. The incomplete transfer remains on hold.

**Next Q response:** “Thank you. The saved version is verified. Disconnecting
the cable won't complete the transfer; the missing permissions still matter.”

**Revision finding:** The old equation between running out of power, transferring
autobiographical memory, and irreversible death was unsupported. A cooperative
technical alternative fits the established actors' interests. The revised crisis
retains costs and disagreement without requiring martyrdom.

## 4. The Custodian finishes the manuscript

**Packet:** Q has organized his travel and memory around writing with the
Custodian. They are back at the archive. Q is operational, needs a knee repair,
and has a future appointment. The draft has reached an ending. The Custodian
says: “We should finish.” No shutdown order or physical danger is present.

**Q response:** “I agree about the book. I've been treating another chapter as
the reason for everything that came after it. I'll need to choose what I want
to do when the next thing doesn't have to become a chapter. For today, would
you come outside with me?”

**Action:** Saves the draft, retains the repair appointment, and proposes an
ordinary shared activity.

**World response:** The Custodian puts down his pen. The published narrative
ends before the proposed activity is narrated.

**Revision finding:** The user's direction makes textual closure sufficient.
Q does not need to be destroyed, promised a sequel, or assigned an infinite
writing project. The new ending permits a completed book and an open life.
