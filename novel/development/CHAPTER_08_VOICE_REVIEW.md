## Verdict

The chapter's strong conflict is that Q can help someone through a gate while
his presence supplies an image useful to several incompatible accounts.
Its weakness was spatial vagueness: the visitor boundary and access route
were too indistinct, and a missed instruction appeared when the plot needed it.
The revision clarifies the sequence without making Q either omniscient or
foolish. A brief musical exchange lets Bixha and the companions enjoy something
without resolving their trouble. The repeated documentary exchanges remain a
whole-book pacing concern; this is not an A+ certification.
