# Chapter Six: a salary, a chair and two jugs

## Verdict

The chapter works best when Chano's appetite for the appointment is inseparable
from his wish to make it useful. Its threat was a municipality so uniformly
false that neither accepting the job nor attempting reform could surprise us.
The revision gives Irma limited useful authority, gives Leticia a labor interest,
and lets a water delivery succeed. Q responds practically to anger and preserves
the difference between a discrepancy and a proven accusation. The chapter is
more credible and less instructional, but still needs the whole-book rhythm
pass; these checks do not establish A+ literary quality.

response at the settlement; limit the ledger's evidentiary claims.

Protect the chair and Chano's back; the workshop gossip and unreliable cousin;
the Custodian asking Q to restore breakfast to the supposed promise.

while Q helps without acquiring the right to turn either into a completed lesson.
