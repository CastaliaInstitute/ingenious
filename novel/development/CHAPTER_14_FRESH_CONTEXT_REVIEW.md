Q has two plans: one has a vehicle conflict, the other eliminates that conflict
but shows only drive time. Neither actual pickup duration nor completeness of
stops has been confirmed. Q has no authority to fund or dispatch the route.

Both responses decline to certify success. Google explicitly asks for pickup
time and a complete stop list; the local response asks Rosa for actual times and
missing stops. Its proposed action awkwardly says to pause the call and then ask
Rosa; that staging is not adopted. The existing scene is already more precise:
Q asks about timed pickups, Rosa brings the driver over, the driver identifies
missing stops, and Chano enters paid extra hours before notifying the schools.
No revision to that sequence is warranted by these outputs.

**Before lunch.** Chano has agreed that both water-delivery outcomes belong in the
book without a forced character arc. Papers are going away. Food stalls are
visible, but no menu or prices are known. No immediate administrative task or
charge emergency competes with the meal. Q can accompany his friends.

Both responses accept that the explanation can wait and propose moving toward
food. Google gives a rather formal priority statement; the local answer offers
to listen but accepts eating first. Its suggested "small smile" is not adopted:
the scene should express Q's warmth through words and movement, not assume a
human facial mechanism. Neither invents a served dish or a completed
conversation. The revised reply is authored, not copied: "Let's see if they've
imagined any of them here." Q closes the notebook. The vendor lifting a pot lid
and the companions following Chano are author-created world actions, not outcomes
validated by either one-step model call. The text does not claim that food charges
Q or invent a human digestive body for him.
