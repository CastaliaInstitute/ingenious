# Sources and limits for the motivation revision

## Eastern Michoacán

The reserve scenes use oyamel forest and a route toward eastern Michoacán,
not an undifferentiated pine forest reached by continuing west from Guanajuato.
These primary sources informed the geographic and ecological correction:

- [CONANP: Reserva de la Biosfera Mariposa Monarca](https://www.gob.mx/conanp/documentos/reserva-de-la-biosfera-mariposa-monarca-209460)
- [CONANP: regional context](https://conanp.gob.mx/conanp/dominios/rbmonarca/region.php)
- [UNESCO: Mariposa Monarca biosphere reserve](https://www.unesco.org/en/mab/mariposa-monarca)
- [UNESCO World Heritage: Monarch Butterfly Biosphere Reserve](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1290/)

The assembly, foundation, named participants, damaged trees, hose, and software
demonstration are fictional. A Mazahua-speaking elder in this scene does not
represent every community in Michoacán. A child's account of his grandmother
is a family story, not evidence of a uniform regional belief. The revised
itinerary is not yet a verified road map or a dated travel schedule.

## Pierre Menard

The seminar uses an interpretive premise: identical words can acquire different
significance through changed authorship and circumstances. It does not claim
that experience automatically improves a text. Q's joke about finding an excuse
to keep a bad sentence makes that distinction explicit.

- [Borges Center: “Can Pierre Menard be the author of Don Quixote?”](https://www.borges.pitt.edu/sites/default/files/0816.pdf)
- [Borges Center: further critical discussion](https://www.borges.pitt.edu/sites/default/files/1204.pdf)

The dialogue is newly written, not a quotation from Borges. Its behavioral
plausibility remains an editorial judgment; literary criticism is not a test
of an autonomous agent.

## The 2012 calendar scene

The previous draft incorrectly paired December 21, 2012 with **4 Ajaw,
8 Kumk'u**, the Calendar Round associated with the creation date. The
Smithsonian's National Museum of the American Indian gives the 2012 date
as **4 Ajaw, 3 K'ank'in** and distinguishes a completed Long Count cycle
from an apocalyptic prediction. Q now catches that mismatch in Castalia's
fictional publicity script instead of confidently teaching the error.

- [Smithsonian NMAI: The Calendar System](https://maya.nmai.si.edu/calendar/calendar-system)
- [Smithsonian NMAI: The Meaning of 2012](https://maya.nmai.si.edu/2012-resetting-count/meaning-of-2012)
- [INAH: Sala Mexica del Museo Nacional de Antropología](https://www.inah.gob.mx/foto-del-dia/sala-mexica-del-museo-nacional-de-antropologia)

The Sun Stone belongs to the museum's Mexica gallery. The revised fiction
does not place it in a small display case or attribute Castalia's invented
apocalypse banner to the museum's own curators. Noemí and Nadia are fictional
characters. Noemí's family and radio anecdotes are not ethnographic evidence
or claims to describe all contemporary Maya communities.

## Invented institutional and technical events

Viento Común, its procurement terms, the visitor incident, the kiosk failure,
the university certificate, and the sealed-deposit arrangements are fictional.
The revised power relationship is contractual supply through transmission
infrastructure and the grid, not an assertion that a dedicated stream of
identifiable electrons travels exclusively from the Isthmus to Amealco.
The storage module is encrypted and Q does not have its keys. Neither the
technical incidents nor the institutional disputes are reports of actual events.
