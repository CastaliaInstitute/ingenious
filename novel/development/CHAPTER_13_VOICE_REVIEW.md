## Verdict

Rebeca's rehearsal and Alma's comb already permit comedy and affection to escape
the archive's categories. The greatest threat is the narration's habit of
explaining admirable restraint instead of giving people something to do together.
This revision makes the donor encounter alter the budget, gives Lucía actual
questions to answer after the disclosure, and lets Q enjoy a promised coauthor
call. It strengthens the chapter without certifying A+ literary quality.

## Revision brief

Three major revisions, in order: negotiate the visit and paid work; dramatize the
family's questions; give the coauthors an exchange they want to continue.

Protect Rebeca's performance pause and claim for payment; Ofelia's two styles of
capital letter; Alma's comb, private deposit and living sister in the later coda.

After revision the archive is a place people work, quarrel and joke, with Q among
them rather than above them as their interpreter.

Minor repairs: specify Amealco and approved classroom visits; remove the absurd
assurance that Q did not investigate the photographer's home; replace the account
of Alma's chair as non-symbol with her loosening a shoe and Q's ordinary diary reply.
